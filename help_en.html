📘 General User Guide 📷 Live Camera & High-FPS Tutorial

Live Object Detection & Auto-Labeling Suite

Complete Desktop Guide for Real-Time Vision, Model Zoo Inference & Instant Dataset Annotation

1. Quick Start

  1. Launch Application: Double-click LiveAnyLabeling.exe or the desktop shortcut.
  2. Open Camera Dock: Click the green Live Camera button on the left toolbar or open View -> Live Camera Dock.
  3. Select Camera & Resolution: Choose Camera 0 (or your external webcam) and select 1080p FHD or 4K UHD.
  4. Select AI Model: In the Model Zoo dropdown, choose any YOLO model (YOLOv8, YOLOv10, YOLO11, RT-DETR) or use one of the pre-loaded local models.
  5. Start Streaming: Click Start Camera. Real-time bounding boxes, labels, confidence scores, and telemetry will appear on the live feed!

2. The Three Viewing Modes

1. Integrated Canvas Mode

Streams live video directly inside the primary annotation canvas. Allows instant Freeze/Snapshot to convert any live frame into editable polygon/bounding box annotations, plus optional automated interval saving.

2. Continuous Monitor HUD

Clean, high-FPS surveillance and monitoring view with full on-screen telemetry (Camera FPS, Inference FPS, Latency in milliseconds), active class filter checklist, and fast model switching.

3. Dual-Pane (Side-by-Side)

Displays the active camera feed in the left pane while keeping the frozen/captured editable annotation canvas on the right for simultaneous live monitoring and ground-truth dataset refinement.

3. Freeze-to-Label Workflow

Turn live video into high-quality training datasets in seconds:

  • Press Space or click 📸 Freeze / Snapshot to Label at any time.
  • The live frame pauses and all detected AI bounding boxes and polygons are instantly loaded into the shape list!
  • You can click and drag boxes, rename labels, add new shapes, or refine polygons.
  • Press Ctrl + S to save your annotations in YOLO (.txt), COCO (.json), or Pascal VOC (.xml) formats.
